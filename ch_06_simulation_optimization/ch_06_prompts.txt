CHAPTER 6 – SIMULATION AND OPTIMIZATION MODEL SPECIFICATION PROMPTS

============================================================ PROMPT 1:
MONTE CARLO SIMULATION – IRR
============================================================

Objective

Simulate the Internal Rate of Return (IRR) for a three-year project by
incorporating uncertainty in annual cash flows.

Project Structure

Time Horizon: 3 years

Initial Investment (Year 0): Fixed cash outflow = -100,000

Cash Flow Distributions

Year 1 Cash Flow: Distribution: Uniform Minimum: 20,000 Maximum: 40,000

Year 2 Cash Flow: Distribution: Normal Mean: 50,000 Standard Deviation:
5,000 Constraint: Cash flow >= 0

Year 3 Cash Flow: Distribution: Triangular Minimum: 40,000 Most Likely:
60,000 Maximum: 80,000

Simulation Settings

Number of simulations: 10,000

Outputs

For each simulation: - Calculate IRR based on the simulated cash-flow
path

Summary statistics: - Mean IRR - Median IRR - Confidence intervals

Visualizations

    - Histogram of IRR outcomes
    - Box plot highlighting spread and outliers
    - Probability density plot
    - Cumulative distribution function (CDF)

============================================================ PROMPT 2:
MONTE CARLO SIMULATION – PROJECT DURATION
============================================================

Objective

Estimate the distribution of total project completion time by simulating
uncertainty in phase durations.

Project Structure

Project composed of three sequential phases: - Data Preparation -
Analysis and Review - Final Delivery

Phase Duration Distributions

Phase 1 – Data Preparation: Distribution: Triangular Minimum: 5 days
Most Likely: 8 days Maximum: 12 days

Phase 2 – Analysis and Review: Distribution: Normal Mean: 15 days
Standard Deviation: 3 days Constraint: Duration >= 0

Phase 3 – Final Delivery: Distribution: Uniform Minimum: 4 days Maximum:
10 days

Simulation Settings

Number of simulations: 10,000

Outputs

For each simulation: - Total project duration (sum of all phase
durations)

Summary statistics: - Mean total duration - Median total duration - 90th
percentile duration

Visualizations

    - Histogram of total project duration
    - Cumulative distribution function (CDF) plot

Interpretation

This simulation supports probabilistic planning statements such as: -
“There is a 75% chance the project finishes within 32 days.” - “To be
90% confident, we should plan for at least 35 days.”

============================================================ PROMPT 3:
MONTE CARLO SIMULATION – PROFIT UNDER UNCERTAIN DEMAND
============================================================

Objective

Estimate the distribution of monthly profit under uncertain customer
demand.

Pricing and Cost Structure

Selling Price per Unit: Fixed at 50

Cost per Unit: Fixed at 30

Inventory Available: Fixed at 1,100 units

Demand Distribution

Monthly Demand: Distribution: Normal Mean: 1,000 units Standard
Deviation: 200 units Constraint: Demand >= 0

Simulation Settings

Number of simulations: 10,000

Business Logic

Units Sold: min(demand, inventory)

Profit Calculation: Profit = (units_sold * price) - (inventory * cost)

Outputs

    - Distribution of profit outcomes
    - Probability of a loss (profit < 0)
    - Summary statistics (mean, median, percentiles)

Visualizations

    - Histogram of profit outcomes
    - Box plot emphasizing downside risk
    - Cumulative distribution function (CDF) showing probability
      that profit falls below key thresholds

============================================================ PROMPT 4:
OPTIMIZATION – TWO PRODUCT PRODUCTION
============================================================

Objective

Determine the optimal production quantities of Products A and B in order
to maximize total profit, subject to labor and material constraints.

Products and Profit

Product A: Profit per unit: 40

Product B: Profit per unit: 30

Resource Requirements

Labor: Product A: 2 hours per unit Product B: 4 hours per unit Total
labor available: 100 hours

Material: Product A: 3 units per unit Product B: 2 units per unit Total
material available: 90 units

Decision Variables

A = number of units of Product A to produce B = number of units of
Product B to produce

Constraints

Labor constraint: 2A + 4B <= 100

Material constraint: 3A + 2B <= 90

Non-negativity: A >= 0 B >= 0

Objective Function

Maximize total profit: 40A + 30B

============================================================ PROMPT 5:
OPTIMIZATION – THREE PRODUCT PRODUCTION
============================================================

Objective

Determine the optimal production quantities of Products A, B, and C in
order to maximize total profit, subject to labor, material, and
production minimum/maximum constraints.

Products and Profit

Product A: Profit per unit: 25

Product B: Profit per unit: 40

Product C: Profit per unit: 35

Resource Requirements

Labor: Product A: 1 hour per unit Product B: 3 hours per unit Product C:
2 hours per unit Total labor available: 160 hours

Material: Product A: 4 units per unit Product B: 3 units per unit
Product C: 5 units per unit Total material available: 200 units

Production Constraints

Minimum production: Product A: at least 10 units

Maximum production: Product C: at most 40 units

Decision Variables

A = number of units of Product A to produce B = number of units of
Product B to produce C = number of units of Product C to produce

Constraints

Labor constraint: A + 3B + 2C <= 160

Material constraint: 4A + 3B + 5C <= 200

Minimum production: A >= 10

Maximum production: C <= 40

Non-negativity: A >= 0 B >= 0 C >= 0

Objective Function

Maximize total profit: 25A + 40B + 35C

============================================================ PROMPT 6:
OPTIMIZATION – TRANSPORTATION PROBLEM
============================================================

Objective

Determine shipment quantities from two warehouses (W1, W2) to three
stores (S1, S2, S3) in order to minimize total shipping cost while
meeting store demand and not exceeding warehouse capacity.

Shipping Cost per Unit

From W1: W1 -> S1: 4 W1 -> S2: 6 W1 -> S3: 8

From W2: W2 -> S1: 5 W2 -> S2: 4 W2 -> S3: 7

Supply (Warehouse Capacity)

W1 capacity: up to 120 units W2 capacity: up to 150 units

Demand (Store Requirements)

S1 demand: 80 units S2 demand: 100 units S3 demand: 70 units

Decision Variables

x11 = units shipped from W1 to S1 x12 = units shipped from W1 to S2 x13
= units shipped from W1 to S3 x21 = units shipped from W2 to S1 x22 =
units shipped from W2 to S2 x23 = units shipped from W2 to S3

Constraints

Warehouse capacity constraints: x11 + x12 + x13 <= 120 x21 + x22 + x23
<= 150

Store demand constraints: x11 + x21 = 80 x12 + x22 = 100 x13 + x23 = 70

Non-negativity: x11, x12, x13, x21, x22, x23 >= 0

Objective Function

Minimize total shipping cost: 4x11 + 6x12 + 8x13 + 5x21 + 4x22 + 7x23
